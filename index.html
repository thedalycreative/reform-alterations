    Good to know
    
    
    Do I need an appointment to drop off?
    Yes. RE:FORM is a home-based studio in Belmont, so drop-offs and fittings are by appointment. Send a quote request and we'll find a time.
    
    
    When do I pay?
    Payment is due before garments are collected or delivered. Specialist or high-value projects, including bridal work, may need a deposit.
    
    
    Will the price change after I approve the quote?
    Not for the work you approved. If you ask for changes after approval, any extra work is quoted before we do it. And if we find the job needs a different price once we start, for example because of hidden construction or a fabric issue, we'll contact you to agree it before going any further.
    
    
    What if something isn't right?
    If work wasn't completed as agreed, we'll assess it and put it right at no additional labour charge.
    
    
    Can you work with bridal, beaded or vintage pieces?
    Yes, within reason. Delicate, embellished and vintage garments are quoted individually after an inspection, with a timeline agreed up front. If a piece is too fragile or complex to alter safely, we'll tell you honestly.
    
    
    Do you work with businesses?
    We welcome enquiries from boutiques, formalwear and bridal retailers, and corporate wardrobe programs. Get in touch to talk about regular work.
